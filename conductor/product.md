# Initial Concept

Native iOS app in SwiftUI with a list of virtual tables, trip planner and timetables for MHD in Bratislava.

## Vision
To provide a seamless, native, and feature-rich public transport experience for Bratislava (MHD) users, leveraging the latest iOS capabilities like SwiftUI, Live Activities, and Dynamic Island.

## Target Audience
- Daily commuters in Bratislava.
- Occasional users and tourists navigating the city's public transport system.
- Users who value native iOS experiences and integration (Widgets, Live Activities).

## Core Features
- **Virtual Table:** Real-time departures for bus, tram, and trolleybus stops.
- **Trip Planner:** Intelligent routing between locations in Bratislava.
- **Timetables:** Offline and online access to vehicle schedules.
- **Maps:** Visual representation of stops and routes.
- **Live Activities & Dynamic Island:** Real-time tracking of trips and departures directly from the lock screen and Dynamic Island.
- **Apple Watch Support:** Quick access to departures and trip info on the wrist.

## Goals
- Provide highly accurate and timely transport information.
- Offer a polished, native iOS UI/UX.
- Ensure high performance and responsiveness.
- Support offline functionality for core features (timetables, routing).
