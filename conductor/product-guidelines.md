# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- Professional and concise.
- Friendly and helpful tone.
- Focus on clarity and ease of understanding for all users.

## Branding
- Use Bratislava's transport system colors where appropriate (e.g., Red, Blue).
- Maintain a modern, clean visual language.
- Consistent use of icons (SF Symbols) across the app.

## UX Principles
- Native First: Leverage standard iOS design patterns and SwiftUI components.
- Responsiveness: Ensure all interactions are fast and fluid.
- Information Density: Balance detail with readability, prioritizing key info (e.g., departure times).
- Accessibility: Adhere to iOS accessibility guidelines (Dynamic Type, VoiceOver).
